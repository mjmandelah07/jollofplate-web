# JollofPlate Brand Identity

## Brand name

**JollofPlate**

## Tagline

**Every Plate Tells a Story.**

### Alternative taglines

- Home of Premium Jollof
- Fresh. Hot. Authentic.
- More Than Just Rice.
- Where Jollof Comes Alive.
- Made Fresh, Served Hot.

## Mission

To serve authentic, delicious, and freshly prepared jollof meals that bring people together through exceptional taste, quality ingredients, and a seamless digital ordering experience.

## Vision

To become Africa's most loved digital-first jollof food brand by combining authentic flavors, exceptional customer service, and innovative technology that makes ordering food effortless.

## Core values

1. Freshness
2. Quality
3. Consistency
4. Customer First
5. Speed
6. Cleanliness
7. Innovation

## Brand personality

Warm, vibrant, authentic, modern, premium, and community-driven. Every meal is prepared with care, using quality ingredients and traditional recipes while embracing modern technology for convenience.

## Target audience

- Students
- Young professionals
- Families
- Office workers
- Corporate lunch orders
- Event catering customers
- Food lovers

## Voice & tone (UI copy)

- Warm and inviting, never stiff
- Clear and direct for CTAs (`Add to Cart`, `Order via WhatsApp`)
- Premium but approachable — celebrate food, not jargon
- Prefer short supporting lines under headlines

## Visual direction for the web

- Mobile-first layouts
- Food photography as the main visual anchor (real plates, steam, color)
- Use brand colors for CTAs and accents; keep backgrounds warm cream
- Avoid generic purple SaaS gradients and dark-mode-first marketing pages
- Hero: brand + one headline + one short line + CTA + dominant food image
